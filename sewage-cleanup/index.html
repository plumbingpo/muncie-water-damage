A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Muncie Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Muncie, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

Common Sewage Cleanup Questions

Temporary relocation is what we usually recommend, particularly for a backup that contaminated a large part of the home. Airborne pathogens and the chemicals used for decontamination can both pose health risks. Our recommendation will depend on what we find in your particular home.
A blocked sewer line on the property is the most common starting point. Typical culprits are tree roots entering the pipe, grease hardening inside it and wipes or similar items flushed down a toilet. Older lines can also crack or collapse, and heavy rain can overload the municipal sewer and push sewage back through floor drains. To know for certain, have the line camera-inspected once cleanup is finished.
Removing the sewage and decontaminating the property is usually done in 1 to 3 days. If materials have to be replaced, full restoration can stretch to 1 to 3 weeks, based on how much was damaged. We prioritize getting your property safe for occupancy as quickly as possible.
In Muncie, a sewage cleanup usually runs $2,000 to $10,000 or more, and the total reflects how large the affected area is and how far contamination spread. Small contained backups may cost $1,500 to $3,000, while extensive sewage flooding can exceed $15,000 including material replacement and reconstruction.
Some things can be kept and others cannot. We can generally clean and disinfect non-porous belongings, for example glass, metal, hard plastic and tile that is sealed. Anything porous the sewage touched must go, which covers mattresses, upholstered furniture, drywall, insulation, carpet and padding. We document what is removed so you have a record for your insurance claim.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
