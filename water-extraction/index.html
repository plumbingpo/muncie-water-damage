Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Across Muncie, Muncie Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

How We Handle Water Extraction

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle water extraction from start to finish:

1

Fast Arrival and Setup

Our extraction crew arrives quickly with truck-mounted and portable equipment ready for immediate deployment. After a quick look at the situation, extraction starts without delay.

2

Standing Water Removal

We use truck-mounted extraction to clear high volumes of water standing on carpet, flooring and surfaces. Deep water in a crawl space or basement is pumped out with submersible pumps.

3

Deep Extraction from Materials

Wall cavities, carpet fibers, padding, upholstery and awkward corners are extracted using tools made for the purpose. Every bit removed by machine now shortens the drying time later.

4

Transition to Drying

After extraction has done all it can, structural drying begins with commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers placed around the area. We take moisture readings at this point, which become the baseline for tracking how drying proceeds.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Add our number, (608) 313-2013, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Professional extraction also addresses moisture that you cannot see or reach with consumer tools. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
